About Us

Relational

Through out weekly meals, we get to know each other deeply. Visitors aren't just a face in the crowd.

Liturgical

The liturgy, or structure, of the service may feel familiar to folks with a certain kind of church background. It's not complicated, and anyone can participate (or not) just by following along in the worship booklet or Book of Common Prayer.

Traditional

Sermons and teaching focus on Jesus, the Bible, and orthodox Christian doctrine.

Prayerful

As we seek to follow our Shepherd, our first and best work is always done through prayer.

Our Diocese

The Church of the Great Shepherd is proud to be part of the Anglican Diocese of Pittsburgh within The Anglican Church in North America (ACNA).

About the Diocese

The Anglican Diocese of Pittsburgh serves over 100 parishes and missions across western Pennsylvania, northern West Virginia, and northwestern Maryland. We are committed to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ through worship, service, and mission.

Visit the Diocesan Website →

The Anglican Communion

We are part of the worldwide Anglican Communion, one of the largest Christian communions in the world with over 85 million members across 165 countries. Our tradition traces back to the early church and embraces both Catholic and Reformed theology.
