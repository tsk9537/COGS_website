Welcome! We're glad you're here.

❓ Where are you?

You'll find us in an office complex, across from the police station and the local driving school. Look for our signs!

⏰ When is Our Worship Service?

Sunday morning services start at 10am and lasts about 90 minutes. Special worship services are held during the Lenten and Christmas Seasons and will be announced. The potluck lunch starts around 12pm.

🍽️ Wait, there's lunch?

There's lunch, and you don't even have to come to church to join us. We share food, company, and life. You can meet new friends or catch up with old ones. We make extra food so there's always enough for guests.

Dietary Accommodations

Are you gluten-free, nut-free, keto, vegetarian, or something else? We probably are too. Dishes are labeled so you can choose wisely.

First Visit Tips

  • Parking: Guest parking is available in the front of the building
  • Check-in: Stop by the welcome center for a visitor gift
  • Children's Programs: Age-appropriate classes for children through 5th grade
  • Accessibility: We are a wheelchair accessible facility
