Children & Youth

Children ages 4-10 can participate in Children's Church during the sermon. Young visitors are also welcome to stay with their parents and use one of our busy bags or coloring sheets clipboards. We believe the sounds of young voices and hands don't take away from the life of the church - they add to it.

🚌 Transportation Options

Not sure how to get here? We offer multiple ways to reach us, including public transit, rideshare services, and driving. For more details on all transportation options, visit our Transportation Page →.

🚆

Metra - UP West Line

The Metra Union Pacific West Line runs from downtown Chicago through the western suburbs, including Wheaton. The Wheaton station is located at 402 W. Front St, walking distance from our church.

  • Taxi/Rideshare: About a 1 minute ride from Wheaton Station
  • Walking: Approximately 12 minutes
🚌

Pace Bus

Pace Suburban Bus serves the Wheaton area with several routes connecting to Metra stations, shopping centers, and other key destinations in DuPage County.

  • Route 301: Runs along Roosevelt Road, travels to and from the Wheaton Metra Station. From the station, the church is a 0.6-mile walk
