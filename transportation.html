Transportation Options

🚆 Metra - UP West Line

About the UP West Line

The Metra Union Pacific West Line runs from downtown Chicago through the western suburbs, including Wheaton. The Wheaton station is located at 402 W. Front St, walking distance from our church.

Getting to Church from Wheaton Station

From the Wheaton Metra station, it's approximately .6 miles to Church of the Great Shepherd at 903 West Liberty Drive. You can:

  • Taxi/Rideshare: About a 1 minute ride
  • Walking: Approximately 12 minutes

Sunday Service Times from Chicago

To arrive for our 10:00 AM worship service, we recommend taking an early morning train. The UP West Line typically has trains departing Union Station as early as 6:00 AM on Sundays.

Schedule & Fares

Return Trip from Church

If you plan to take the train home after church, check the UP West Line schedule for return trips from Wheaton station. The last trains typically depart around 10:00 PM on Sundays.

🚌 Pace Bus

About Pace Bus Service

Pace Suburban Bus serves the Wheaton area with several routes connecting to Metra stations, shopping centers, and other key destinations in DuPage County.

Routes Near Our Church

  • Route 301: Runs along Roosevelt Road, travels to and from the Wheaton Metra Station. From the station, the church is a 0.6-mile walk

Sunday Service

Pace bus service on Sundays may have reduced frequency. We recommend checking the Pace schedule in advance for Sunday morning times to ensure you can arrive for our 10:00 AM worship service.

Fares & Payment

Pace accepts cash, exact change, Pace Ticket Book, and Ventra Card. Day passes are available for unlimited rides on a single day.

Schedule & Trip Planner

For the most up-to-date schedule, route maps, and trip planning tools, visit:

Visit PaceBus.com →

Ventra Card

Pace participates in the Ventra payment system. You can purchase a Ventra Card online or at select retail locations. Contactless payments are also accepted on buses.
